③ What happens when a salesperson taps "Save Sale"

Only the sale itself is written. Points, stock and dashboards are calculated from it, so they always match.

1📱 Salesperson fills the saleshop + products + price + quantity → taps Save
2🌐 Sent over the internet (HTTPS)with their login cookie, so the server knows who it is
3⚙️ app.py checks itlogged in? correct role? date not in the future? quantities and prices valid? missing price? → otherwise it's refused
4🗄️ Saved in sales.db1 row in orders (who, which shop, date) + 1 row per product in order_items (qty, price)
🌶️ Pointsrewards.py recalculates the shop's balance from all its orders, redemptions and adjustments
📦 Stockleft = stock moves − packs sold − free packs, for this salesperson
📊 Dashboardsmonth totals, zero-sale days and the calendar are added up from orders
5📱 Phone shows the result"+20 points", "अभी लेना है या बचाना है?", the WhatsApp button (the message is sent from the phone's own WhatsApp and is not stored on the server)

④ Stored vs calculated

Numbers that are calculated can't go wrong or drift: they are re-added from the saved records every time you open a screen.
